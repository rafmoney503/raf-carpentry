## Design (redesign, October 2026: "drafting paper")
- **Look**: light paper background, pencil-grey text, one blueprint-blue accent, square 2px corners, photos on a white mount with a hairline border and soft shadow.
- **Colours** (Tailwind tokens in src/app/globals.css `@theme`): paper #f1f2ef, raised #e6e7e2, mount #fbfbf9, ink #16191c, muted #4f555b, faint #5e646a, accent #2547d0 (hover #1b38ad). Use `bg-paper`, `bg-raised`, `text-ink`, `text-muted`, `text-faint`, `border-line`, `border-line-strong`, `bg-accent`, `text-accent`, `text-on-accent`. Do not use hex colours or zinc/gray classes in pages.
- **Fonts** (self-hosted in src/fonts, loaded in src/app/layout.tsx with next/font/local): Bricolage Grotesque for headings (`font-display`), Geist for body (`font-sans`), Geist Mono for small labels and measurements (`font-mono`).
- **Shared pieces**: `.btn .btn-primary .btn-ghost .btn-sm`, `.link-more`, `.kicker`, `.mount` (globals.css); `Container`, `PageHeader`, `SectionHeading`, `MountedImage` (src/components/ui.tsx); `QuoteCta` (src/components/QuoteCta.tsx, quote panel used on home and contact).
- **Homepage**: src/app/page.tsx + src/app/home.css, content from content/pages/home.json (editable in TinaCMS).
- **Rules (taste-skill)**: no em-dashes in new copy, no emoji icons, one accent colour, one "Get a quote" label for every quote button, max 20 words under the hero headline.
- **Logo**: Crisp black R on white circle (public/images/r-logo-final.png).
- **Quotes go through ServiceM8**: every "Get a quote" button (navbar, hero, quote panels) opens the ServiceM8 booking page in a new tab. The link, phone and email live in src/lib/site.ts (QUOTE_URL).
- **TinaCMS status (3 Oct 2026)**: live. Raf edits at /admin and every save is a "TinaCMS content update" commit to main, so ALWAYS `git fetch` / rebase onto origin/main before pushing. Needs NEXT_PUBLIC_TINA_CLIENT_ID and TINA_TOKEN in Vercel; scripts/build-cms.mjs skips the editor build without them. Blog posts have a `category` field in the schema (Projects, SketchUp, Cabinetos, Tips).
- **Social icons**: src/components/SocialIcon.tsx (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook marks from Simple Icons, CC0, `fill="currentColor"`; `networkOf(urlOrLabel)` picks the network). Used in the Footer, /ms (round icon buttons), About buttons and the blog post "Enjoyed this post?" buttons. Any new social link should get its icon too.
- **How it works (3 Oct 2026)**: /how-it-works, content in content/pages/how-it-works.json (TinaCMS collection "How it works", tina-lock.json regenerated by running `TINA_PUBLIC_IS_LOCAL=true npx tinacms dev` for ~10 s). Sections: five steps (Get in touch + the home page Survey/Draw/Build/Fit text), What I need from you / What you get from me, Materials in plain English (painted MDF, moisture-resistant MDF, birch ply, walnut and oak, IKEA PAX built in; cost shown as £ to ££££ only, never prices; photos and links from real jobs), finishes, a brief builder (src/app/how-it-works/brief-builder.tsx: chips and inputs become a ready-written message sent by WhatsApp, email or copy; the choices live in the JSON so Raf can edit them), FAQ (no kitchens), CTA. Only states things the site already promises (24 h reply, free quote and visit, 3D drawing signed off first, 18 mm board incl. backs, scribed fit, room left clean): no deposits, timescales or guarantees, as Raf has not given them. Linked from every quote panel (QuoteCta), contact page, home process section, job page quote band, footer, mobile menu and /ms. No floating WhatsApp button on this page.
- **Calculator (3 Oct 2026)**: /calculator, "Workshop calculator in mm". Same keys and behaviour as the CabinetOS Quick calc (cabinetos-site src/components/CalcPopup.tsx + src/lib/calc.ts): keypad, typing on a keyboard, ( ), %, history (localStorage `raf_calc_history`, last 8), copy. Extras: live answer while typing, cm / m / inches (nearest 1/16") / feet-inches, a bar against a 2440 x 1220 board, five example sums with 18 mm board. - **WhatsApp (3 Oct 2026)**: `WHATSAPP_NUMBER`, `whatsappUrl(text)` and `whatsappText(path, title)` in src/lib/site.ts build wa.me links with the first message already typed (job pages name the job, blog posts name the post, others ask for area/postcode and what they need). Shown as: floating button on every page except /ms and /editor (src/components/WhatsAppFloat.tsx, reads the page h1), "WhatsApp me" ghost button beside every "Get a quote" (src/components/WhatsAppButton.tsx, QuoteCta and job pages), "WhatsApp" line on contact and home quote section, footer link, and a card on /ms (in ms.json, so editable in TinaCMS). ## Project pages (added October 2026)
- **Source photos**: Raf drops each job into a folder on his Mac: Documents/Raf Carpentry Projects/<YYYY-MM what area>/ with subfolders 1 Finished, 2 Build steps, 3 Drawings, 4 Free plan, plus notes.txt (template folder inside; area only, never client names).
- **Processing**: photos are rotated, stripped of all EXIF/GPS, resized to 1600 px and lightly corrected (white balance, levels). Short iPhone clips give stills; longer clips become muted web videos (720 px wide, H.264) in public/videos/.
- **Data**: one JSON per job in content/projects/<slug>.json (title, area, finished YYYY-MM, type, tags (search words and filter buttons), summary, intro, cover, facts, steps, gallery, optional compare / plan (image, real, imageLabel, realLabel, views) / video / planSale (previews, includes, price, buyUrl Payhip link or freeDownload) / blogSlug / tools). Loader: src/lib/projects.ts.
- **Pages**: /portfolio lists jobs (newest first) plus an "Earlier work" strip from content/pages/portfolio.json; /portfolio/[slug] shows main photo with a title-block of facts, one interactive piece (before/after slider, plan vs real, or video), numbered build steps, photo grid with a full-screen viewer, optional free SketchUp plan / blog link / tools, quote band and next job.
- **Search**: src/lib/search.ts (stem + matches) shared by the My Work page and its quick-filter buttons.
- **Components**: src/components/project/CompareSlider.tsx, ProjectVideo.tsx, ProjectGallery.tsx, Lightbox.tsx (+ lightbox.css, shared full-screen viewer), PlanCard.tsx (free preview + buy/download), PlanDrawings.tsx (plan next to the real thing, plus a 'More drawings' row from plan.views; all drawings open full size), ScreenGrid.tsx (SketchUp screenshots); styles in src/app/portfolio/project.css.
- **Selling plans**: Payhip (London, free plan 5% + Stripe 1.5% + 20p). Each plan £4.99, bundle planned at £12.99. Add `planSale` to the job JSON with preview images (no dimensions) and the Payhip link; the job page and /sketchup pick it up.
