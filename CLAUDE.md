- **Timber Stack game (9 Oct 2026, Raf's idea)**: /tools/timber-stack (src/app/tools/timber-stack/: page.tsx, timber-stack-game.tsx, opengraph-image.tsx with public/images/tools/timber-stack.jpg). The aim (Raf, 9 Oct): stack as much timber as possible, until almost nothing is left to put on top. A board slides in from the right; tap, click, Space or Enter drops it; the part hanging over the board below is cut off and falls (sawdust, a saw sound), so the next board is that much narrower ('only 45 mm left' under 80 mm); within 8 mm counts as Perfect (snaps square), and from the third Perfect in a row each one grows the board back 20 mm. Each board is 47 mm sawn timber: a yellow builder's tape measure up the left edge shows the height (mm ticks, cm numbers, a blue box every metre), and dashed height marks (seat 450 mm, worktop 900, door 1981, ceiling 2400, upstairs ceiling 4800, roof ridge 7500, ten metres) give the top board 40 mm back when passed, with a chime; under the score: boards, height and 'Door height in 17'. Speed starts at 300 mm/s and rises 6 per board to 720. Missing the stack ends the game; the view steps back to show the whole stack and tape above the score card (boards and height, Play again, Share score with the height, Get a quote). A new game starts by itself 4 seconds later ('New game in 3', Raf asked 9 Oct 2026); tapping outside the card starts it at once; pressing Share, or the page going into the background, stops the countdown. Units are mm across a 1,000 mm area, first board 600 mm, a dimension line shows the width; timber changes every 6 boards (birch ply, oak, walnut, pine, sapele). Canvas, no library; sounds made in code with a mute button; best score kept in the browser (localStorage `raf_timber_best`, `raf_timber_mute`). On its own page so it fills a phone screen (no floating buttons there, LayoutShell); linked from a full-width 'Free game' card on the Tools page under the calculator and tips cards (line drawing 'stack' in sketches.tsx) and the sitemap.
