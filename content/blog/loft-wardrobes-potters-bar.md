A brand new loft room in Potters Bar, with the roof coming down along one wall and a wide window on the other. The room needed four things: wardrobes under the slope, a window seat with storage, a TV unit and a dressing table.

## Drawn first, then taped to the wall

Before cutting anything I drew every unit in CabinetOS, my own app. Each unit printed as a sheet: a 3D drawing with its sizes, then its cut list. I taped them to the wall and worked through them one by one.

The sheets also print the sizes the app has checked against the drawing, like a shelf at 962 mm for a 964 mm space less 2. Every line gets a tick as it is cut.

![A size check on a printed sheet, ticked](/images/blog/loft-wardrobes-potters-bar/sheet.jpg#narrow)

## Three units down the slope

Everything starts on a timber base frame, levelled on the floor. The first unit next to the door is a full height wardrobe, 1039 mm wide, 1980 mm high and 590 mm deep, with double hanging.

![The first carcass on its base frame](/images/blog/loft-wardrobes-potters-bar/base.jpg) ![The angled unit next to it](/images/blog/loft-wardrobes-potters-bar/slope.jpg)

*The wardrobe on its base, then the angled unit beside it.*

Then the roof takes over. The angled unit next to it is 1980 mm on the left and 1340 mm on the right, with two shelves over four drawers. The small unit at the end comes down from 1290 to 820. The loft hatch is in that wall, so its back is cut round the hatch: open the door and the hatch is still there.

![The small unit with the loft hatch inside](/images/blog/loft-wardrobes-potters-bar/hatch.jpg) ![Shelves over four drawers](/images/blog/loft-wardrobes-potters-bar/inside.jpg)

*The loft hatch kept inside the small unit, and the shelves over the drawers.*

## Shaker doors, two layers

The doors are shaker style made the simple way: a 12 mm MDF base the size of the door, with a 6 mm border glued on top. The doors on the angled units are cut to follow the slope.

![The finished wardrobes](/images/blog/loft-wardrobes-potters-bar/doors.jpg#narrow)

## The window seat

Under the window, three seat boxes of 1069 mm each, 400 mm high and 500 mm deep, run along the wall and turn the corner, all with shaker cupboard doors. At the end is a TV unit with the sockets inside it. I tested the seat myself.

![The window seat](/images/blog/loft-wardrobes-potters-bar/seat.jpg) ![Two cupboards open](/images/blog/loft-wardrobes-potters-bar/seat-open.jpg)

*The seat along the window and round the corner, and two of its cupboards open.*

Last came a dressing table with a drawer, on timber legs, under the other slope by the radiator. Everything is left bare MDF, ready for the decorator.

![The dressing table](/images/blog/loft-wardrobes-potters-bar/table.jpg#narrow)
