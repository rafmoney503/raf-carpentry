The reception desk is the first thing visitors see in this office in Brentwood. It is L-shaped, with a raised counter on the visitor side, a slatted front, and a matching unit to one side.

## Getting it level

The desk sits on plinth frames. I built those first and set them out on the floor, packed level. The line of the desk was marked on the carpet with tape, and the carcasses went on following that line.

![Plinth frames set out along a tape line on the carpet](/images/blog/reception-desk-brentwood/1.jpg) ![The L-shaped top fitted across both runs](/images/blog/reception-desk-brentwood/2.jpg)

## The top and the finish

The L-shaped top runs across both sides of the desk. The panels and slats were painted before the final fit, which keeps the edges between them clean.

![The slatted front of the desk](/images/blog/reception-desk-brentwood/3.jpg) ![Shelves on the staff side](/images/blog/reception-desk-brentwood/4.jpg)

On the staff side there are shelves under the counter, so the desk top can stay clear.
