A front bedroom in Chiswick, with an alcove each side of the chimney breast. There was a white wardrobe in one alcove and an oak desk with shelves round the chimney breast. The client wanted the whole wall in birch ply: a wardrobe in each alcove, and the chimney breast between them made into the wall behind the bed.

![Before: a white wardrobe and an oak desk round the chimney breast](/images/blog/two-birch-ply-wardrobes-chiswick/before.jpg#narrow)

*Before: the old wardrobe and desk.*

## Drawn first

Before anything was cut I drew it in 3D, in two versions: one with two columns of drawers in each wardrobe, one with a single column of four drawers on the outer side. The client could open the doors on their phone, look inside both, and pick. They went with the single column, and that is what got built.

## Back to the walls

The old furniture came out first, so both alcoves and the chimney breast were back to bare plaster, with the old fireplace still open at the bottom.

![The empty alcoves and the chimney breast after the old furniture came out](/images/blog/two-birch-ply-wardrobes-chiswick/stripped.jpg) ![A timber base frame levelled in the empty alcove](/images/blog/two-birch-ply-wardrobes-chiswick/base.jpg)

*Back to bare walls, then a level base frame in each alcove.*

In each alcove a timber base frame goes down on the floorboards and gets levelled first. Old floors are rarely flat, and the wardrobe needs something true to stand on, or the doors never line up.

## Boxes on the floor, then up

Each carcass goes together lying on the floor and is squared up with clamps, then stood up onto its base. The top edges are scribed to the ceiling, so there is no gap where the house isn't straight.

![A birch ply carcass assembled on the floor and held square with clamps](/images/blog/two-birch-ply-wardrobes-chiswick/carcass.jpg) ![Laser lines inside a carcass marking the shelf positions](/images/blog/two-birch-ply-wardrobes-chiswick/laser.jpg)

*Built square on the floor, then the shelves set out to the laser.*

Inside, every shelf and drawer runner is set out with the laser level, so the lines are level and the two wardrobes match each other.

## Doors and drawers

Each wardrobe has two doors: a full height one next to the chimney breast, and a shorter one on the outer side over a column of four drawers. There are no handles anywhere. Every door and drawer front has a round finger hole instead, which keeps the front of the birch ply clean.

![The left wardrobe with its doors and drawer fronts on](/images/blog/two-birch-ply-wardrobes-chiswick/doors.jpg#narrow)

## The chimney breast

Between the wardrobes the chimney breast is faced in birch ply from the floor to the ceiling, with the wiring brought through for two wall lights. The lower part covers the old fireplace, with a ledge along the top of it and a finger slot at the bottom so it can come off.

![The chimney breast faced in birch ply between the wardrobes](/images/blog/two-birch-ply-wardrobes-chiswick/breast.jpg) ![Two shelves on black straps going up over the chimney breast](/images/blog/two-birch-ply-wardrobes-chiswick/shelves.jpg)

*The birch ply facing, then the shelves on their straps.*

Over it go two birch ply shelves hung on black straps, and a black reading light on each side.

![A black wall light under the shelves](/images/blog/two-birch-ply-wardrobes-chiswick/light.jpg#narrow)

## Inside

The wardrobes are mirror images. In each one the outer column has adjustable shelves over the four drawers, and the column by the chimney breast has a hanging rail over low shelves, with a long shelf across the top of both.

![The left wardrobe open](/images/blog/two-birch-ply-wardrobes-chiswick/open-left.jpg) ![The right wardrobe open](/images/blog/two-birch-ply-wardrobes-chiswick/open-right.jpg)

*Shelves over drawers on the outer side, hanging by the chimney breast.*

![The four drawers pulled out](/images/blog/two-birch-ply-wardrobes-chiswick/drawers.jpg#narrow)
