Short answer: in London I build IKEA PAX in from £1,050 a metre of wall + VAT (£1,260 with VAT). That is my work and the MDF: fitting the frames and the fillers, plinth and top round them, scribed to your walls. IKEA's parts are on top, and on a plain white PAX they come to about £230 a metre.

## A 2.4 metre wall, worked out

Say the wall is 2,400 mm wide and the ceiling 2,400 mm high. The frames that fit it best are a 100, a 75 and a 50, all 236 cm high, with 78 mm of filler at each end ([the sums](/blog/fitting-ikea-pax-wall-to-wall)). At that ceiling height there is room for a strip above them, not a top box ([why](/blog/ikea-pax-height-for-your-ceiling)).

**My part:** 2.4 m at £1,050 is £2,520 + VAT, so £3,024 with VAT.

**IKEA's part,** plain white, at the prices on IKEA's UK website on 21 November 2026:

- **Frames:** a 100 (£90), a 75 (£80) and a 50 (£75): £245.
- **FORSAND doors with hinges, 229 cm high:** four 50 cm doors at £56 and one 25 cm door at £46, because the 75 frame takes a 50 and a 25: £270.
- **Inside:** a rail and a shelf in each frame, about £40.

That comes to about £555 with VAT, before handles and IKEA's delivery.

**Together:** about £3,580 with VAT for a 2.4 metre wall of wardrobes, floor to ceiling.

IKEA changes its prices, so check yours in IKEA's PAX planner when you finish your design.

## What's in my price

- Fitting the PAX frames and hanging IKEA's doors.
- A base, levelled on the floor, with an MDF plinth along the front.
- A filler at each end, scribed to the wall with a [school compass](/tips#scribe-with-a-compass).
- A strip or a top box up to the ceiling, scribed to it.
- All the MDF ready for your decorator to paint.

## What isn't

- **IKEA's parts.** You order them and have them delivered, so the choice of doors and insides is yours.
- **Painting.** The plinth, fillers and top are bare MDF for your decorator.
- **Handles.** IKEA sells them separately from the doors.

## What changes IKEA's part

- **The doors.** FORSAND is the plainest. A panelled BERGSBO door is £71 and a GRIMO £81, each 50 by 229 cm with hinges (same date). On the wall above, the four 50 cm doors in BERGSBO add £60, and in GRIMO £100.
- **The insides.** A 100 cm KOMPLEMENT drawer is £32. Swap shelves for drawers and that part grows quickly.

## Against made to measure

My price for a plain made-to-measure wardrobe is £1,200 a metre + VAT (£1,440 with VAT), with everything in it and no IKEA basket. For the same 2.4 metre wall that is from £3,460 with VAT. [Work out your own width](/cost/fitted-wardrobes).

So on my invoice plus IKEA's, the two come out about the same. The difference is the painting. Made to measure is bare MDF, so your decorator paints the whole wardrobe. With PAX, IKEA's frames and doors come finished, and only the plinth, fillers and top need paint. That is where PAX saves you money.

Made to measure still wins where PAX does not fit: shallow alcoves, sloping ceilings, beams, or a wall that leaves big fillers. [PAX or made to measure?](/blog/ikea-pax-or-made-to-measure-wardrobe) goes through it.
