This small room in Chiswick had to work as a bedroom and a study. Everything in it is birch ply: a tall wardrobe on one side, cupboards running overhead, and a desk hung on the wall under the window, with a drawer and a wall lamp.

## The wall came first

Before any furniture, the outside wall was insulated. Insulation boards went in between battens, the joints were taped, and then the wall was lined. The room is warmer for it, and everything fixes into something solid.

![Insulation boards between battens on the outside wall](/images/blog/birch-ply-room-chiswick/1.jpg) ![Birch ply cut to size on a bench in the room](/images/blog/birch-ply-room-chiswick/2.jpg)

## Cut and built in the room

I set up a bench in the room and cut the birch ply to size right there. The walls were lined in ply, then the overhead cupboards and the desk went in.

## Making a small room work hard

The wardrobe has hanging space, shelves and drawers. The desk has a drawer that pulls out under the top, so the desk itself can stay clear.

![Inside the wardrobe: hanging space, shelves and drawers](/images/blog/birch-ply-room-chiswick/3.jpg) ![The desk with its drawer pulled out](/images/blog/birch-ply-room-chiswick/4.jpg)
