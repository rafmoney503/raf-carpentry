Short one this week. A long wall, a corner, and a client who needed somewhere to sit and somewhere to put things.

This is the drawing the client approved:

![SketchUp drawing of the lift-up storage bench with its sizes](/images/blog/corner-storage-bench-kings-park/drawing.jpg)

## The numbers

- **3000 mm** along the wall
- **1215 mm** round the corner
- **400 mm** high, which is a comfortable seat height
- **400 mm** deep on the long run, **700 mm** on the corner piece

That last one matters. The corner piece is deeper, so it becomes the big box for the bulky stuff.

## Lids

Three lids, each on hinges, each with a finger hole so there is nothing sticking out to catch a leg. Lift one and the whole box underneath is storage.

![One lid up on the long run](/images/blog/corner-storage-bench-kings-park/lid.jpg) ![The corner lid up, with the deep box underneath](/images/blog/corner-storage-bench-kings-park/corner.jpg)

Built in MDF with a simple raised panel on each front, ready for painting.

![Two lids open](/images/blog/corner-storage-bench-kings-park/two-lids.jpg#narrow)
