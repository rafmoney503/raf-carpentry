Every system I build for other businesses started as an evening I lost to paperwork in my own. Enquiries were the first.

An enquiry from the website used to mean copying the same details by hand: into ServiceM8, into a reply, into the diary. After a day on the tools, that's the last thing you want to do, and it's where mistakes creep in.

Now the customer types it once, and nobody types it again.

## What happens now

1. **The customer sends their brief** from the website: what they want, rough sizes, photos of the room. Or a 3D wardrobe they planned themselves.
2. **ServiceM8 fills itself in.** A client, a contact and a Quote job are made, with their photos in the job diary.
3. **They get a thank-you straight away**: an email with three designs like theirs, sent through ServiceM8 and filed on the job.
4. **The design becomes a cut list.** A wardrobe planned on the website arrives as a CabinetOS project with the parts already worked out.
5. **My phone puts it at the top of Today**, above the visits, follow-ups and money owed, in the order things need doing.
6. **The reply is ready; I press send.** Nothing goes until I've read it.
7. **The visit goes in the diary**, booked in ServiceM8's schedule and confirmed on WhatsApp with the message already typed.
8. **Quote, work, paid, reviewed.** Every payment and what's left, then a Google review request ready to send.

The first four happen by themselves. The last four are one tap from me.

## What it changed

The customer gets an answer in seconds instead of that evening. I don't retype anything. And nothing gets lost between the website, my inbox and the diary, because there is only one place it lives.
