I've been on the tools in London for more than 22 years: wardrobes, kitchens, doors, floors. For most of that time the hard part of the job wasn't the job. It was everything around it. The sums before the first cut, the drawings for the client, the evenings with the paperwork.

Rafal Solutions is where I turn those problems into tools.

## How a tool starts

It always starts the same way. Something on a real job costs time or money, and it keeps happening. So I write down every step of it, build something small that does that step for me, and use it on my own jobs until it stops getting in the way.

Only then does it go on this site. Every tool here runs Raf Carpentry before it runs anyone else's business. That's the rule.

## Five steps, one job

Put together, the tools now cover a whole job, from the first 3D click to a fitted wardrobe that's been paid for:

- **Learn**: Build It in 3D teaches 3D from the very first click.
- **Design**: CabinetOS for SketchUp draws wardrobes and cabinets at real board sizes.
- **Make**: CabinetOS turns the design into cut lists, board layouts and bench drawings.
- **Fit**: the Workshop Tools do the sums on site, on your phone.
- **Run**: systems built for your business, joined up with what you already use, like ServiceM8.

Each tool hands the job to the next, so nothing is typed twice.

## Why three of the five are free

Build It in 3D, the Workshop Tools and the CabinetOS free plan cost nothing. Times are hard for a lot of people, in the trade and outside it. A free tool that saves someone an evening, or helps a kid or a grandparent make their first thing in 3D, is worth more out there than sitting behind a price.

The paid parts, the full CabinetOS plans and the systems I build for other businesses, keep the free parts going.
