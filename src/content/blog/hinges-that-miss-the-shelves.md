Every fit has the same small sums. Where do the hinges go on this door? What length of runner fits this drawer? How wide is each door if the opening is this, with this many doors? None of them are hard. All of them take a few minutes, every time, usually standing on a dust sheet with a pencil.

The Workshop Tools do those sums on your phone.

## Door hinge positions

A tall wardrobe door needs more than two hinges. Inside, the shelves are often evenly spaced, and a hinge set out in the usual way can land right where a shelf goes.

The Door hinge positions tool spaces the hinges so each one misses the shelves. You put in the door and the shelf positions, and it gives you the measurements to mark.

## The others in the set

- **Drawer runners**: the runner length and sizes that fit the drawer you're making.
- **Wardrobe unit sizes**: doors, shelves, the hanging rail, and the plinth and infill for a unit that goes to the ceiling.
- **Spirit level**: a level on your phone for when yours is in the van.
